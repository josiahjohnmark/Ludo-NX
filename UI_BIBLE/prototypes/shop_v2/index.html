    The plan

    • A featured stage at the top rotates three offers: the Daily Deal with a timer, the one-time Starter Pack, and the NX Club subscription.
    • Four tabs: Dice, Tokens, Emojis, Energy. Items stand on wooden shelves grouped by rarity, with the best item on a spotlight showcase.
    • Rarity is visible before you read anything: common is calm, rare sparkles, epic glows, legendary shines with rays.
    • Common and rare items cost coins you earn by playing. Epic, legendary, packs, the Starter Pack and NX Club are real-money purchases through RevenueCat.
    • Tap the price to buy. Tap the card to see the item turning on a plinth first. After buying, the price becomes EQUIP.
    • Tokens preview in your house colour, so you see exactly how they will look on the board.

    • Card press

      The card sinks slightly and springs back.

    When you buy

    • Real-money purchase

      A spinner while Google Play's payment sheet is open, then a flash, a burst in the rarity colour, an OWNED stamp, and the price turns into EQUIP.

      flash 100 msburst 600 msstamp 220 ms
    • Coin purchase

      Coins fly out of your coin pill into the card while the number counts down.

      fly 500 mscount 500 ms
    • Not enough coins

      The price shakes, the coin pill flashes red, and a message offers more coins.

      shake 240 msflash 600 ms
    • Energy or coin pack

      Ten bolts or coins arc into the top bar, the number counts up and the pill bounces.

      fly 700 mscount 800 msbounce 320 ms
    • Equip

      The item gets an amber outline and the one you used before loses it.

      instanttoast 2.2 s
